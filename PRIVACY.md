# Privacy Policy

## Safe Paste

Safe Paste is a personal open-source project designed to redact recognised sensitive information from clipboard text before it is pasted.

### Data Collection

Safe Paste does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.

Clipboard content is processed locally on the user's device and is not sent to the developer or any external service.

### Third-Party Services

Safe Paste does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or external data-processing services.

### Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as Safe Paste develops and new features are introduced.

### Contact

For questions or concerns, please open an issue on the Safe Paste GitHub repository.
